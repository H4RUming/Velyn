# Velyn v1.0.0

The first public build of Velyn, an iPhone photo editor that processes photos on the device and preserves imported originals.

## Download and install

**Requires iOS 27 or later and an arm64 iPhone.**

- `Velyn-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa` is the compiled app, including its local models. **You must sign it with your own Apple identity and a provisioning profile that allows your device before installation.** Downloading or opening it in Files does not install it. It is not an App Store or TestFlight build.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you verify the download: run `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt` in the folder containing both files.
- [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/H4RUming/Velyn/blob/v1.0.0/Docs/Releases/INSTALL.md) explains AltStore Classic, Apple Configurator, and Xcode installation. Xcode users can build the `v1.0.0` tag directly; the release source archives are provided below.

**Suggested route: AltStore Classic.** It signs the IPA with your Apple Account; free-account apps need refreshing every 7 days and have a 3-app limit. See the [official AltStore guide](https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-classic/your-altstore). Apple Configurator can install an IPA **after it has been signed for your device**; the unsigned download cannot be installed with Configurator alone. See [Apple's signing instructions](https://help.apple.com/xcode/mac/current/en.lproj/dev23ea8b877.html).

The public bundle ID is `com.h4ruming.velyn`. Signing tools may replace it. Use the same bundle ID and signing identity for later updates to keep access to that installation's data.

## Included in v1

- A photo library with albums, ratings, flags, recoverable trash, batch export, and imports from Photos, Files, or the camera.
- RAW development, exposure and color controls, curves, HSL, grading, detail, crop, and geometry adjustments. Presets, versions, and undo keep edits reversible.
- Local adjustment masks, depth-based lens blur, and object removal with a zoomable paint/erase canvas and an explicit Run action.
- P3 gamut expansion and SDR-to-HDR gain map prediction. HDR gain is applied equally to RGB channels, with midtone protection and color regression checks.
- JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and original-file export, including 16-bit PNG and HDR JPEG/HEIC. The save screen shows dimensions, color space, and output size.
- Histograms, clipping indicators, and HDR metrics, plus English/Korean language settings.

Processing stays on the device. AOT-GAN, Depth Anything V2 Small, and GMNet are bundled. Apple's optional tap selection assets download only when requested. There is no app server, login, analytics, or advertising SDK.

## Known limits

This is an early public build of the current implementation. Broader physical-device testing of RAW variants, camera behavior, display color, sustained heat, and system import/export flows remains in progress. Release acceptance gates and Lightroom feature/quality parity have not been established.

HDR prediction estimates brightness; it cannot recover clipped highlight detail or guarantee the original scene's luminance. Results depend on the image, viewer, display, and available HDR headroom. RAW controls depend on the file's supported decoder. System pickers and permission prompts follow the iOS app language setting.

**Photos stored inside Velyn are excluded from system backups. Keep separate copies of your originals before deleting the app or moving to another installation.** Edited exports remove location metadata; exporting the original preserves its existing metadata.

## Validation

The engine suite passes 71 tests. The iOS Simulator build, unsigned iPhone release archive, and signed development build succeed. The v1 signed build was installed on an iPhone 15 Pro. Its launch check was blocked by the device lock; the preceding development build had passed installation and launch checks. The public IPA is checked for its version, architecture, bundled models/localizations, archive integrity, and absence of a personal provisioning profile or app signature. Installing the public IPA after signing with another account has not been tested.
